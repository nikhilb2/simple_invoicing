Click **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. You only see the items your role allows.

|---|---|---|
| [Company](../getting-started/company-setup) | Everyone | Company details, logo, terms, e-way bills and invoice series. |

| [Security](#security) | Everyone | Your password. |
| [Keyboard Shortcuts](#keyboard-shortcuts) | Everyone | Change the keys. |
| [Appearance](#appearance) | Everyone | Light or dark theme. |

| [Backups](./backups) | Administrators of a self-hosted copy | Create and restore backups. |

## Security

**Settings → Security** changes your password.

1. Enter your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice.
2. Click **Update password**.

The new password needs at least 8 characters, and common passwords such as `password123` are refused. The other items on the checklist are advice, so a longer passphrase is a good option. Changing your password signs you out on other devices but keeps you signed in on this one.

If you signed up with Google, you have no password yet. This page offers **Set a password** so you can also sign in with email.

### Forgot your password?

Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email. If an account exists, you get a link that works once and lasts an hour. Using it signs you out everywhere else.

On a self-hosted copy, this needs a server mail setup. Without one, the link is not shown, so ask your administrator.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

![Keyboard Shortcuts settings](/screens/shortcuts.webp)

**Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts** lists each action with its default and your current key. See [which shortcuts work](../getting-started/tour#keyboard-shortcuts).

1. Click the box under **Your Shortcut**. It shows "Press keys…".
2. Press the new combination, or press Esc to cancel.
3. Click **Save**.

If the combination is already in use, you see which action owns it. **Reset** restores one shortcut, and **Reset All to Defaults** restores all of them. Shortcuts are saved to your account, so they follow you.

## Appearance

**Settings → Appearance** offers **System**, which follows your device, **Light** and **Dark**. The choice is saved only in this browser. The round button in the top bar switches between light and dark.
