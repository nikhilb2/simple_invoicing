# Day Book

**Reports → Day Book** lists every voucher in date order across all ledgers. Use it for reconciliation, or for checking what was entered on a given day.

![The Day book voucher register](/screens/day-book.webp)

## Choose what to see

- Pick a range: **Today**, **This week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Last quarter** or **Financial year**. You can also set **From** and **To** yourself. The page opens on the active financial year.
- **Voucher type** narrows the list to sales, purchases, receipts, payments or credit notes.
- **Find** searches references, ledgers and particulars.

## Read the register

Entries are grouped by date, with columns for **Voucher**, **Ledger**, **Particulars**, **Debit** and **Credit**. Sales and payments are debits. Purchases and receipts are credits. Cancelled documents are left out.

The **Period debit** and **Period credit** at the top always cover the whole date range. The total at the bottom of the register reflects whatever filters you have on.

## Export

**Export PDF** and **Export CSV** download the register.

::: warning Exports ignore two filters
Exports cover the full date range. They ignore **Voucher type** and **Find**, so filter the CSV in your spreadsheet if you need one type.
:::
