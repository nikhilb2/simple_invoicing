# Finding your way around

![The app with the sidebar on the left and the dashboard on the right](/screens/dashboard.webp)

## The sidebar

| Section | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The [dashboard](../reports/dashboard). |
| **Sales** | [Invoices](../sales/invoices), [Invoice Feed](../sales/invoice-feed), [Quotations](../sales/quotations), [Invoice Dues](../sales/dues), [Credit / Debit Notes](../sales/credit-notes) and [Cash & Bank](../sales/cash-bank). |
| **Catalogue** | [Products & Stock](../catalogue/products) and [Produce Items](../catalogue/manufacturing). |
| **Reports** | [Analytics](../reports/analytics), [Day Book](../reports/day-book), [Tax Ledger](../reports/tax-ledger), [GSTR-1 Filing](../reports/gstr1) and [GSTR-3B Worksheet](../reports/gstr3b). |
| **Marketplace** | [Browse, My Listings and Orders](../marketplace/). |
| **Ledgers** | Your [customers and suppliers](../ledgers/). |

Below those are the **Company** and **Financial Year** switchers. At the bottom are [**Settings**](../settings/), your email and role, and **Logout**.

Only one section stays open at a time. The arrow at the top collapses the sidebar to icons, and the app remembers your choice in that browser.

On a phone, the sidebar is hidden. Tap the three-bar button to open it, and tap outside it to close it.

## The top bar

- **Press ? for shortcuts** opens the list of keyboard shortcuts.
- **Help** opens this guide in a new tab.
- **Request a feature or report a bug** takes you to GitHub, where you can open an issue. You need a free GitHub account.
- The round theme button switches between light and dark.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** at any time when you are not typing in a field to see the current list.

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Open Reports (Analytics) | Ctrl+R |
| New Customer (Create ledger) | Ctrl+Shift+C |
| Focus scan box, on the invoice page | Alt+S |
| Go to Dashboard | Alt+O |
| Go to Invoices | Alt+I |
| Go to Quotations | Alt+Q |
| Go to Invoice Dues | Alt+U |
| Go to Credit / Debit Notes | Alt+N |
| Go to Products & Stock | Alt+P |
| Go to Ledgers | Alt+L |
| Go to Day Book | Alt+D |
| Go to Tax Ledger | Alt+T |
| Go to GSTR-1 Filing | Alt+G |
| Go to GSTR-3B Worksheet | Alt+B |

You can change any of these in [Settings](../settings/#keyboard-shortcuts).

The list also shows **New Invoice** (Ctrl+N), **Save Invoice** (Ctrl+S) and **Search** (Ctrl+F), but these do not do anything yet.

Ctrl+R opens Analytics instead of reloading the page. To reload, use your browser's reload button or change this shortcut.

## Dates and links

Most list pages keep their filters and page number in the address. Refreshing or going back keeps your view, and you can bookmark it.

If a page says you need a company, finish [company setup](./company-setup) first. If you open a page you do not have access to, the app returns you to the dashboard.
