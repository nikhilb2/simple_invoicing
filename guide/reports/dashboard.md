# Dashboard

**Overview** is the first page you see after signing in, with a snapshot of sales, money owed and stock.

![The Operations dashboard](/screens/dashboard.webp)

The dashboard has no date or financial-year filter. Figures cover **all dates** for the active company, except where the card says this month or last 12 months. Cancelled invoices are not counted.

## The cards

| Card | What it shows | Click to open |
|---|---|---|
| **Net sales** | Total of all active sales invoices, including GST. Credit notes are not subtracted. | |
| **Outstanding** | What customers still owe on sales invoices. | Invoice Dues |
| **Overdue** | The part of that past its due date. Turns red when above zero. | Invoice Dues |
| **This month** | Sales invoiced since the 1st, and receipts this month. | |
| **Catalog** | How many products you have. | Products & Stock |
| **Stock value** | Quantity × purchase price, across stock-tracked products. | Products & Stock |
| **Low stock** | Products at or below their reorder level, and how many are out of stock. | Low stock list |
| **Avg invoice** | Total sales ÷ number of sales invoices. | |

Large amounts are shortened, such as ₹66.2L. Hover over a figure for the full amount.

## The panels

- **Sales, purchases & receipts**: a monthly chart for the last 12 months.
- **Payment status**: how many sales invoices are paid, part paid and unpaid.
- **Inventory pressure points**: the five tracked products with the least stock.
- **Top products by revenue**: your five best sellers.
- **Latest activity**: the six newest invoices. Click one to preview it.

For figures over a chosen period, use [Analytics](./analytics).
